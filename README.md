# Supply-Chain-Management-Dashboard

A Power BI and SQL dashboard for end-to-end supply chain monitoring, supplier performance, logistics efficiency, and operational quality analysis.

## Overview
This dashboard provides a holistic view of:

supplier performance
operational efficiency
product quality
lead time and defect analysis
revenue impact and operational risk
Business Objective
To get a holistic view of supply chain analysis and business performances across various suppliers, locations, product type, quality and operations.

## KPI Highlights

Total Cost
Quantity Ordered
Average Lead Time in Days
Average Defect Rate
Revenue Loss
Total Revenue
Passed Result
Failed Result
Pending Result

## Tools Used

Power BI
SQL
DAX
Power Query

## Key Features

-Supplier evaluation
-Location-wise performance tracking
-Quality and defect analysis
-Lead time monitoring
-Revenue loss detection
-Operational result analysis

## Business Insights

Supplier performance can be evaluated through cost, lead time, and defect rate
Regional trends reveal operational bottlenecks
Revenue loss analysis highlights financial impact
Quality metrics support better supplier and logistics decisions
